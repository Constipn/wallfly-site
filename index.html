HOW IT WORKS

From your call to clear notes.
In three steps.

  1. 01

    Start your call.

    Open Wallfly when your meeting starts. It captures the audio on your computer. No bot joins, nobody gets an extra invite.

  2. 02

    Jot down what matters.

    A name, a question, a half-finished thought. Your notes tell the AI what is important to you.

  3. 03

    Get clear notes.

    Wallfly combines your notes with the transcript into a summary, decisions and to-dos.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Is Wallfly really free?

Yes. The app is free and open source, with no subscription. The only possible cost: if you choose a cloud AI provider such as OpenAI or Deepgram, that provider bills you for usage. With local models, it costs nothing.

Does it work completely offline?

Yes, in local mode. Transcription runs locally in the OpenWhispr app (installed separately), and summaries run on a local model on your computer. You only need internet once, to download the models.

Where are my notes stored?

In a local library on your computer: notes, transcripts and meeting files. Wallfly has no cloud storage.

Do other participants notice? Do I need their consent?

No bot joins, so nothing shows up in the call. Rules for recording differ by country and company, so let people know you are taking notes.

Does it work with Zoom and Google Meet?

Yes. Wallfly captures the meeting audio on your computer, so it works with Microsoft Teams, Google Meet and Zoom. Automatic meeting detection currently works for Teams (on Mac, through the Accessibility permission). For other apps, start recording manually.

Which transcription and summary models can I use?

Transcription: Deepgram (Nova-3), OpenAI, or Whisper Large V3 Turbo running locally through OpenWhispr.

Summaries and Enhanced Notes: OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), or any OpenAI-compatible API, including local models. Bring your own key; cloud providers may charge for usage.

Which computers are supported? Does it run on Windows?

Wallfly is available for Mac first. It needs Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 26 or later. A Windows version is coming later.
