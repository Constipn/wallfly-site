A FEW MORE DETAILS

Frequently asked questions.

Which transcription and summary models can I use?

Transcription: Deepgram (Nova-3), OpenAI, or Whisper Large V3 Turbo running locally through OpenWhispr.

Summaries and Enhanced Notes: OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), or a custom OpenAI-compatible API, including local models. Bring your own key; cloud providers may charge for usage.

How does Wallfly detect meetings?

When enabled, meeting detection uses macOS Accessibility to spot active Microsoft Teams calls and prompt you to take notes. For other apps, start recording manually.
